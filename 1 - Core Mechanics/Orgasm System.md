\# Orgasm System



The orgasm system is the core resolution mechanic for sexual encounters in ED\&D.



\## Pleasure Points (PP)

\- \*\*Max PP\*\* = Sex Die maximum + Constitution modifier + Sexuality bonus + character level

\- Starts at maximum each dawn (long rest)

\- Short rest: Recover 1 Sex Die + Con mod (once per short rest)

\- PP loss represents building arousal and overstimulation



\## Aroused Condition

\- \*\*Required\*\* for any penetrative sex (Coital, Anal, etc.).

\- Effects: Advantage on Charisma checks targeting the source of arousal; Disadvantage on concentration checks.

\- Creatures without Aroused have \*\*resistance\*\* to pleasure damage (half PP loss).



\## Orgasm Saving Throws ("Le Petit Mort")

When a creature is brought to the \*\*brink\*\* (usually at 50% PP or less, or by specific effects):



\- \*\*DC\*\* = 10 + attacker’s Sexuality bonus + relevant ability modifier

\- \*\*Roll\*\*: d20 + Con modifier + Sexuality bonus

\- Track successes and failures separately (like death saves)



\*\*Outcomes\*\*:

\- \*\*3 Successes\*\*: Edge — retain 1 PP and may continue. Choose to either regain 1 Sex Die + Con mod PP \*\*or\*\* reset failures to 0.

\- \*\*3 Failures\*\*: Climax — roll on the Orgasm Table. Enter Refractory unless special recovery triggers.



\*\*Natural 20\*\*: Immediate recovery — regain 1 Sex Die + Con mod PP and may continue.



\## Orgasm Table

Roll \*\*d100 + Sexuality bonus + Wisdom modifier\*\*:



| Result Range | Intensity       | Effect |

|--------------|-----------------|--------|

| 1–30         | Mild            | Afterglow – Advantage on next ability check (1 minute) |

| 31–70        | Ecstatic        | Gain temporary HP = level + SEX; inspired for 1 hour |

| 71–99        | Transcendent    | Cast one Eromancy spell of level ≤ SEX/2 without using a slot |

| 100+         | Divine Rapture  | Regain full PP + gain 1 use of Legendary Resistance |



\## Refractory Period

After climax (unless recovered via natural 20 or special feature):

\- Immune to all pleasure damage

\- Cannot gain the Aroused condition

\- Cannot climax again



\*\*Ending Refractory\*\*:

\- Once per encounter: Make a Sexuality check (DC 20). Success ends Refractory and regains 1 PP.

\- Otherwise ends on short or long rest.



\## Special Rules

\- \*\*Shared Pleasure\*\*: Some features (e.g., Shared Ecstasy) allow linking PP pools in group scenes.

\- \*\*Edged Condition\*\*: At 50% PP or less → forced Orgasm Saving Throws on further pleasure.

\- \*\*Voluntary Edge\*\*: High Endurance checks can allow characters to hold climax intentionally.



> This system is the mechanical heart of all erotic encounters. It is fully compatible with Sexual Histories and Carnal Classes.
