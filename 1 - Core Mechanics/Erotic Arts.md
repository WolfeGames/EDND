# Erotic Arts

Erotic Arts are broad buckets that group related sexual abilities.  
Characters roll using the appropriate bucket + relevant ability modifier + Sexuality bonus.

## Current Erotic Arts (9 Buckets)

1. **Seduction** (Charisma)  
   Flirtation, dirty talk, eye contact, gestures, building desire, granting the Aroused condition.

2. **Erotica** (Intelligence)  
   Knowledge of anatomy, history, aphrodisiacs, tantric theory, erotic literature, crafting oils/toys, identifying kinks.

3. **Erogeny** (Wisdom)  
   Sensing and stimulating erogenous zones with precision. Covers all forms of touch-based stimulation (manual, oral, penetration, toys, etc.).

4. **Exhibitionism** (Charisma)  
   Public displays, stripping, erotic performance, drawing attention, crowd control.

5. **Restraint** (Dexterity or Intelligence)  
   Bondage, cuffs, ropes, knots, sensory restraint, safe binding, holding positions.

6. **Endurance** (Constitution)  
   Delaying climax, breath control, marathon stamina, resisting overstimulation.

7. **Dominance** (Charisma or Strength)  
   Commanding presence, verbal dominance, power play, imposing will, making partners submit.

8. **Submission** (Wisdom or Constitution)  
   Yielding, service-oriented acts, bottoming, enduring for pleasure, following commands, subspace.

9. **Ritual** (Intelligence or Wisdom)  
   Ceremonial sex, tantric energy work, sex magic, symbolic/intentional intimacy, invoking deities through pleasure.

## Penetration Rules
- **Base DC**: 10 (same size, Coital) / 15 (same size, Anal)  
- **Size Modifier**: +2 per size category the penetrator is larger than the receiver  
- Roll using **Erogeny** or **Endurance** (player's choice) + Sexuality bonus  
- Advantage if partner is Aroused or properly lubricated

## Toolkits & Bonuses
- **Lubricant**: -3 to Anal DC  
- **Erotic Oils**: +1d4 pleasure or advantage on specific checks  
- **Silk Rope**: +2 to Restraint checks  
- **Tongue Ring**: +1d4 pleasure on Lingus-style actions (under Erogeny)

## Gaining Proficiencies
- Sexual Histories grant 2–3 Erotic Arts  
- Carnal Classes grant additional proficiencies and potential Expertise  
- Feats can grant new proficiencies or Expertise in existing ones
