\# Monsters: Astrani (Deva)



\*\*Large celestial, lawful good (or lawful neutral for deities of overwhelming passion)\*\*



\*\*Armor Class\*\* 18 (radiant aura of perfection)  

\*\*Hit Points\*\* 204 (24d10 + 72)  

\*\*Speed\*\* 30 ft., fly 90 ft.



\*\*Sexuality Bonus\*\* +6  

\*\*Fertility\*\* +8  

\*\*Beauty Class\*\* 26 (+8)



\*\*Skills\*\* Erotica +11, Erogeny +12, Seduction +14, Exhibitionism +14  

\*\*Condition Immunities\*\* Aroused (by mortal sources only)  

\*\*Senses\*\* truesight 120 ft. (perceives desire and hidden kinks)  

\*\*Languages\*\* all, telepathy 120 ft.  

\*\*Challenge\*\* 16 (sexual encounter only)



\## Sexual Traits



\*\*Divine Allure\*\*  

Any creature within 60 feet that can see the Astrani has disadvantage on saving throws against its Seduction checks or Eromancy spells. The Astrani may cast \*suggestion\* at will (no components) to encourage acts of love, beauty, or pleasure.



\*\*Pleasure Aura\*\*  

Any creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Astrani must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom save or become overwhelmed with divine desire. On a failure, the creature is charmed for 1 minute and can only use its action to approach the Astrani and offer worship through touch or intimacy. The creature may repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.



\*\*Celestial Embrace\*\*  

The Astrani may touch or embrace one willing creature. The target regains 100 Pleasure Points, removes all exhaustion gained from sexual activity, and automatically rolls on the Orgasm Table with advantage, gaining the highest possible benefit (Transcendent or Divine Rapture).



\*\*Healing Climax (Recharge 5–6)\*\*  

When the Astrani climaxes with a willing mortal partner, the partner regains 100 hit points and 100 Pleasure Points, ends all Refractory periods within 60 feet, and gains temporary immunity to pleasure damage for 1 hour.



\*\*Innate Eromancy\*\*  

The Astrani’s spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 22). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components (often substituting sexual contact):  

\- At will: \*calm emotions\*, \*detect desires\* (as detect thoughts, but only erotic thoughts)  

\- 3/day each: \*greater restoration\* (via prolonged embrace or climax)  

\- 1/day: \*mass ecstasy\* (as mass healing word but restores PP instead of HP)



\## Legendary Actions (Sexual Only)

The Astrani can take 3 legendary actions during an intimate encounter:



\- \*\*Touch of Bliss.\*\* The Astrani deals 36 (8d8) psychic pleasure damage with a touch and forces an immediate Orgasm Saving Throw (DC 20).  

\- \*\*Divine Gaze (Costs 2 Actions).\*\* One creature within 60 ft must succeed on a DC 22 Charisma save or become Aroused and charmed for 1 minute, regarding the Astrani as the embodiment of their ultimate desire.  

\- \*\*Radiant Caress.\*\* The Astrani restores 50 Pleasure Points to one creature it can touch.



\## Description

Astrani (commonly called Deva by mortals) are angels of divine love, beauty, fertility, and transcendent ecstasy. They appear as impossibly flawless humanoids with luminous skin, flowing hair, and wings of pure light. Their mere presence awakens profound desire; their touch brings enlightenment, healing, and overwhelming pleasure.



An Astrani on the Material Plane usually has a sacred mission: inspire great works of erotic art, bless a destined union, guide mortals to divine ecstasy, or punish those who suppress natural desire. They are compassionate yet uncompromising — mortal taboos mean nothing to them.



Union with an Astrani is said to be a life-changing spiritual experience, often leaving mortals forever changed (and occasionally bearing celestial offspring).



\*\*Note for Character Creator\*\*  

Astrani are extremely powerful sexual encounters. They radiate such perfection that refusal is rare. Their climaxes are described as visions of paradise itself.
