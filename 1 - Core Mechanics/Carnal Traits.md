\# Carnal Traits



Carnal Traits are special qualities that define a character's sexual nature, strengths, and quirks.  

They are granted primarily by Sexual Histories, Carnal Classes, Species, and certain feats.

\## How to Use

\- Sexual Histories grant 1–3 Carnal Traits at character creation.

\- Carnal Classes may grant additional traits at specific levels.

\- Feats can grant or improve traits.

\- Humans may choose one extra Carnal Trait from their Sexual History.
