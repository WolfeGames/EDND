# Core Mechanics

## Ability Scores (Carnal Interpretation)
- **Strength**: Raw physical power, dominance, lifting/pinning, deep thrusting
- **Dexterity**: Flexibility, grace, precision of touch, rhythmic motion
- **Constitution**: Stamina, endurance, fertility, resistance to overstimulation
- **Intelligence**: Erotic creativity, anatomical knowledge, tantric theory, improvisation
- **Wisdom**: Reading partner's desires, sensing erogenous zones, pacing pleasure
- **Charisma**: Seduction, presence, commanding group dynamics

## Sexuality Bonus (SEX)
Scales exactly like Proficiency Bonus:
- Levels 1–4: +2
- Levels 5–8: +3
- Levels 9–12: +4
- Levels 13–16: +5
- Levels 17–20: +6

Added to: Erotic Art checks, Orgasm Saving Throws, Fertility rolls, pleasure damage, carnal spell DCs.

## Sex Die
Determines base Pleasure Point maximum and recovery. Chosen from Sexual History or Carnal Class (d6 to d12).

## Pleasure Points (PP)
- **Max PP** = Sex Die maximum + Con modifier + Sexuality bonus + level
- Refreshes fully on long rest
- Short rest recovers 1 Sex Die + Con mod (once per short rest)

## Key Conditions
- **Aroused**: Required for penetrative sex. Advantage on Cha checks toward the source, disadvantage on concentration.
- **Edged**: At 50% PP or less → begins Orgasm Saving Throws.
- **Refractory**: After climax (unless recovered). Immune to pleasure, cannot gain Aroused. Ended with DC 20 Sexuality check.

## Orgasm Saving Throws
- DC = 10 + attacker's SEX + relevant mod
- d20 + Con mod + SEX
- 3 successes → edge or recover PP
- 3 failures → roll on Orgasm Table

## Orgasm Table
Roll d100 + SEX + Wis mod for post-climax effects:
- Afterglow, Ecstatic, Transcendent, or Divine Rapture

## Fertility & Conception
- Fertility = Con mod + SEX + racial/trait modifiers
- Conception: Both partners roll d20 + Fertility vs DC 15
