\# Positions \& Equipment



\## Positions

Positions function like weapons: you make an attack roll (d20 + Str or Dex + Sexuality bonus) to deal pleasure damage (PP loss).



\### Position Tiers

\- \*\*Basic\*\* – Available to everyone. Simple, reliable positions.

\- \*\*Advanced\*\* – Require training (granted by most Sexual Histories and Carnal Classes).

\- \*\*Exotic\*\* – Highly specialized or situational (granted by specific Histories/Classes).

\- \*\*Improvised\*\* – Spontaneous locations (tavern table, alley wall, carriage, etc.). Usually higher DC but flavorful.



\### Position Examples (by Tier)

\*\*Basic\*\*  

\- Missionary, Doggy Style, Spooning, 69, Oral



\*\*Advanced\*\*  

\- Lotus, Reverse Cowgirl, Standing, Pile Driver, Full Nelson, Tantric Chair



\*\*Exotic\*\*  

\- Silken Suspension, Harem Pile, Altar Offering, Ritual Binding, Draconic Coil



\*\*Improvised\*\*  

\- Tavern Table, Alley Wall, Hayloft, Carriage Seat, Bath Tub



\### General Rules

\- Attack: d20 + Str/Dex + Sexuality bonus

\- Damage: Position die + modifier (deals Pleasure Point damage)

\- No proficiency in a tier → Disadvantage on attack roll and half damage

\- Penetration DCs still apply separately (size-based)
