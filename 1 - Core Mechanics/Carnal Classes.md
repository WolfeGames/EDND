\# Carnal Classes



Carnal Classes represent your long-term erotic development and mastery.  

They advance in parallel with your regular character level (1–20) and are designed to feel "chunky and meatier" than Sexual Histories.



\## Core Design Rules

\- \*\*Parallel Progression\*\*: You choose \*\*one\*\* Carnal Class. It gains features at levels 1, 6, 10, 14, 18, and 20.

\- \*\*Carnal Facet\*\*: Every regular D\&D class grants a unique sexual perk when combined with a Carnal Class.

\- \*\*Subclasses\*\*: Chosen at level 3 (2–3 options per Carnal Class).

\- \*\*Feats \& Traits\*\*: Available at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 19 from the Universal Carnal Traits list or new erotic feats.

\- \*\*Focus\*\*: Primarily erotic/roleplay power with light combat crossover. Sparse features to avoid bloat.



\## Currently Promoted Carnal Classes (8 Core)

1\. \*\*Courtesan\*\* – Social mastery, wealth through charm, mass seduction, guild power.

2\. \*\*Divine Consort\*\* – Sacred sex, divine magic via intimacy, fertility and healing.

3\. \*\*Eroticist\*\* – Scholarly mastery, crafting erotic items/spells, anatomical perfection.

4\. \*\*Cult Seducer\*\* – Forbidden rites, mind control through pleasure, summoning.

5\. \*\*Harem Tender\*\* – Group dynamics, jealousy management, orchestrating multiple partners.

6\. \*\*Otherworldly Lover\*\* – Celestial, fey, or patron-bonded intimacy and boons.

7\. \*\*Chosen Mate\*\* – Deep soul-bonded connections, mono or poly romantic mastery.

8\. \*\*Xenophile\*\* – Mastery of interspecies and exotic anatomy pleasure.



\## Carnal Facet Examples (by Regular Class)

\- \*\*Barbarian\*\*: Primal Surge – Add Rage damage bonus to pleasure dealt while raging.

\- \*\*Bard\*\*: Inspirational Moans – Bardic Inspiration dice can be spent on ally Orgasm Table rolls.

\- \*\*Cleric\*\*: Divine Ecstasy – Channel Divinity can restore PP instead of HP in a 30-ft radius.

\- \*\*Druid\*\*: Wild Shape (Carnal) – Retain Erotic Arts proficiencies in beast forms.

\- \*\*Fighter\*\*: Second Wind (Carnal) – Bonus action: regain PP equal to 1d10 + level + SEX.

\- \*\*Paladin\*\*: Lay on Hands (Pleasure) – Can restore PP or grant Afterglow instead of HP.

\- \*\*Rogue\*\*: Sneak Attack (Tease) – Extra pleasure damage on finesse erotic attacks; target disadvantaged on next Orgasm Save.

\- \*\*Sorcerer\*\*: Arousal Metamagic – Sorcery Points can empower carnal spells or force Aroused condition.

\- \*\*Warlock\*\*: Eldritch Pleasure – Eldritch Blast can deal psychic pleasure instead of force damage.

\- \*\*Wizard\*\*: Skin Spellbook – Scribe spells on skin; cast while nude without material components.



\## Feat \& Trait Progression

At levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 19 you may choose:

\- A new Carnal Trait from the universal list

\- An erotic-specific feat (to be defined later)

\- Expertise in one Erotic Art



\## Future Expansion Notes

\- Each Carnal Class will have 2–3 subclasses.

\- Spellcasting: Some classes (Divine Consort, Eroticist) gain limited Eromancy spell progression.

\- Goal: Keep features focused on erotic flavor and utility with minimal combat bleed.
